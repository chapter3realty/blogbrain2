## Wording that must never appear on a page

The sources do not support any of these.

1. That Fannie Mae will approve or grant an exception for a project. A lender may only request one (C13).
2. That Limited Review is still available, or that it ended on March 18, 2026 (C1, C2).
3. That Fannie Mae has no limits on investors or rentals in a condo, or does not care how many units are rented. A new project still needs 50% of units sold to owner-occupants or second-home buyers (B4-2.2-02). The single-entity limits still apply (C6). Fannie Mae treats 75% or more investment and second home ownership as a red flag for a condo hotel (B4-2.1-03). CPM can restrict a project to certain occupancy types (C10).
4. A 10% reserve rule stated with no date after January 4, 2027, or a 15% rule stated as current before that date (C9).
5. That every two- to ten-unit condo project has its review waived. A five- to ten-unit project inside a master association needs Full Review (C3).
6. That a name with "hotel", "motel" or "resort" always makes a condo ineligible (C4).
7. That a second home can never be rented, or that rental income from a second home can be used to qualify (S2, S3).
8. That the 4% South Carolina ratio applies without an application, or that the 6% rate is in 12-43-220(a) (S6).
9. That flood insurance is required only in A and V zones. CBRS and OPA areas also require it (F3).
10. That flood insurance covers the land, or covers all personal property (F2).
11. That "every lender" is bound by 12 CFR 339. That rule binds FDIC-supervised banks. Name it as the rule for federally regulated lenders (F1, F8).
