In Horry County for 2026, a conventional loan can go up to $832,750 on a one-unit home (a house, townhouse or condo) before it becomes a jumbo loan. An FHA loan can go up to $541,287. A VA loan has no set limit if you have your full VA entitlement. Here's what each limit means when you're buying in Myrtle Beach and around the Grand Strand. The 2027 limits are usually announced in late November and December, and we'll post them here when they're out.

These are limits on the loan amount, not on the price of the home. You can buy a more expensive home and still stay under a limit, because your down payment covers the difference.

Limits for two- to four-unit homes are higher. Ask a loan officer if you're buying a duplex or larger.

What is a conforming loan limit?

It's the largest loan Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac will buy. Their federal regulator, the FHFA, sets it each year. For 2026, it's $832,750 in most of the country, including Horry County.

A loan at or under the limit is a conforming loan, the standard conventional mortgage. A loan above it is a jumbo loan.

FHA loan limit in Horry County

FHA sets its limits county by county, based on local home prices. Horry County sits at the national minimum, called the floor: $541,287 for a one-unit home in 2026. Georgetown County, including Pawleys Island and Murrells Inlet, is at the same $541,287.

On a home priced above that, an FHA loan still works if your down payment covers the rest. If it doesn't, a conventional loan, with its higher limit, is usually the next option to compare. See our FHA loans.

VA loan limits

If you have your full VA entitlement, there's no VA loan limit. You can borrow as much as a lender approves you for, as long as the appraisal supports the price. If part of your entitlement is still tied up in another VA loan, the county's conforming limit, $832,750 in Horry County, sets how much of your new loan VA will guarantee. Above that, you may need some money down. A loan officer can work out your remaining entitlement. More on VA home loans and VA loans on Myrtle Beach condos.

Over the limit? Your options

A home on the water, a big new build or a high-end condo can push a loan past $832,750. That's where a broker helps, because jumbo programs differ a lot from lender to lender. The usual choices:

  • A jumbo loan. One loan for the full amount, with its own credit, cash and reserve rules. See our jumbo loans.
  • A larger down payment to bring the loan back under $832,750, so it stays conforming.
  • Two loans. A first mortgage at the conforming limit, plus a second loan for the rest.

Which one works best depends on your cash, your income and the home. We run the numbers on each one side by side, so you can see the difference before you decide.

An example. A couple is buying a home near the Intracoastal Waterway in North Myrtle Beach. Their loan would come out a little above $832,750. They compare three paths: a jumbo loan, a bit more money down to stay conforming, or a first and second mortgage. Seeing all three together is the only way to know which costs them less.
