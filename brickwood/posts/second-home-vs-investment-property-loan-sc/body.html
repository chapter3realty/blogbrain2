It comes down to how you'll use the home. It's a second home if you'll stay there part of the year and keep control of it, even if you rent it out some weeks. It's an investment property if you won't stay there, a rental company controls the bookings, or you need the rent to qualify for the loan.

Can I rent out my second home?

Yes. You can rent out a second home and still keep a second home loan. Two things have to stay true. You stay there yourself for part of the year. And the rent isn't used to qualify you for the loan.

What you can't do is hand control to someone else. Listing the house yourself is fine, and so is hiring a manager, as long as you decide when the house is booked. Signing up for a rental program that decides when you're allowed to stay is not. That turns it into an investment property.

Why the label matters

The same house can get two very different loans:

  • Down payment. With a Fannie Mae loan, an investment property needs a bigger down payment than a second home when you buy.
  • Cost. Fannie Mae adds a charge to every investment property loan, and to some second home loans. It shows up in the rate or as a fee at closing.
  • Rent. On an investment property, the rent can help you qualify. A DSCR loan can even qualify you on the rent.

Your exact down payment depends on your credit, the price and the loan, so a loan officer gives you that number when you pre-qualify.

Second home mortgage requirements

For a second home loan sold to Fannie Mae, the home must:

  • be lived in by you for some part of the year (there's no set number of days);
  • be a single house or condo, not a duplex or larger;
  • be livable all year;
  • be under your control, with no rental program or manager deciding who stays there;
  • not be a timeshare.

Buying a condo? The building has its own rules too. See condo loan requirements.

A second home costs more in property tax

South Carolina gives your main home a break. Your legal residence is assessed at 4% of its value, if you apply for it. A second home or rental is assessed at 6%. So the same house comes with a bigger tax bill when it's your second home. Plan for that in your budget.

Be honest about how you'll use it

On your loan application, you say how you'll use the home. Calling a rental a second home to get better terms is a false statement on a loan application, and that's a federal crime. Plans can change later in life. If you want to turn your second home into a full-time rental down the road, talk to your loan officer first.

Already own a home with equity? You can also buy a second home with a cash-out refinance.
