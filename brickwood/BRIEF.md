## Who these pages are for

Buyers who are close to applying and bring a larger loan or more assets: second-home buyers, buyers of coastal condos, retirees moving to the coast, and buyers with savings but little monthly income. The pages pick that audience by topic only. No page says or suggests that anyone is unwelcome, and the Equal Housing Lender line stays on every page (fair lending, `brickwood/LEGAL.md`).

### 1. Condo loan rules after the August 2026 changes

- Title tag: `Condo Mortgage Requirements in SC: The 2026 Rule Changes` (56)
- H1: Did the 2026 Condo Loan Changes Make Your Building Harder to Finance?
- URL: `/condo-mortgage-requirements-south-carolina/`
- Searches: condo mortgage requirements, Fannie Mae condo rules 2026, limited review eliminated, condo reserve requirement, warrantable condo
- Reader: a buyer with a coastal condo picked out, often from out of state, who heard the building "might not qualify".
- Kind of page: news and rule explainer. Lead with what changed, when and for whom, then a before and after table.
- Deciding fact: the text of Fannie Mae Lender Letter LL-2026-03 and the current Selling Guide B4-2 sections: Limited Review retired for applications dated on or after August 3, 2026; the waiver widened to projects of 10 units or fewer; the investor concentration limit; the reserve rule and its date.
- Must answer: what changed and when; does it apply to my loan (primary, second home, investment); what makes a building fail now (critical repairs over $10,000 a unit, special assessments, reserves, commercial space over 35%, single-entity ownership, hotel operations); what FHA requires instead; what loan works when the building fails; what the buyer does before the offer.
- Offer: "Send us the building name. We check it against the new rules before you make an offer."
- Links to: BrickWood loan programs, Myrtle Beach and North Myrtle Beach pages. Across to Chapter3's `/invest/non-warrantable-condos/` and `/invest/condotel-financing/` with the affiliate disclosure.

### 2. Second home or investment property

- Title tag: `Second Home vs Investment Property Loan in South Carolina` (57)
- H1: Second Home or Investment Property? How Lenders Classify Your Beach House
- URL: `/second-home-vs-investment-property-loan-sc/`
- Searches: second home mortgage requirements, second home vs investment property mortgage, can I rent out my second home, beach house loan
- Reader: someone who owns a home and is buying a place at the beach they will use and may rent out part of the year.
- Kind of page: decision guide. Lead with "Usually a second home if..., an investment property if...", then a decision table.

- Links to: BrickWood home purchase, DSCR, and the existing post on using a cash-out refinance to buy a second home. Across to Chapter3 `/buyers/second-home/`, which owns the "where to buy" question.

## Rules for these pages that differ from Chapter3

- BrickWood is a lender. It may describe its own loan programs. It still states no rate, APR or payment amount in a blog post: a rate or payment is a trigger term under Regulation Z (12 CFR 1026.24) and needs the full disclosure, and the site's own rate tables already disagree with each other. Link to one rate page instead.
- A down payment percentage is also a trigger term under 1026.24(d). Until BrickWood's compliance contact decides, the pages state no down payment percentage at all. They say what changes the down payment ("an investment property needs a larger down payment than a second home") and offer to give the reader their own figure. Owner question 1 in `brickwood/LEGAL.md`.
- No "guaranteed", "pre-approved" as a claim to the reader, "lowest rate", "no closing costs" or "government loan" for a non-government program.
- Reverse mortgage copy follows the HUD and CFPB rules on reverse mortgage advertising: the borrower still pays taxes, insurance and upkeep; the loan comes due; counseling is required. No "no payments ever".
- The Chapter3 voice rules apply: short sentences, no em dashes, no metaphors, every fact from a verified row.
